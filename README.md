# Cognifyz Technologies – Data Analyst Internship

My Data Analysis Internship Experience 🚀

During my internship at Cognifyz Technologies, I worked on a variety of engaging and practical data analysis tasks aimed at strengthening my analytical thinking and hands-on experience in data science. This document summarizes the tasks I completed, the tools I utilized, and the insights I gained throughout the internship.

Cognifyz Technologies is a forward-thinking company in the field of data science, known for delivering innovative solutions in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics. It provides diverse services, products, and training programs that support business growth and professional development.

Tasks Completed
Level 1: Fundamental Data Analysis

Top Cuisines Analysis

Identified the three most frequently occurring cuisines in the dataset.
Calculated the percentage of restaurants offering each of these cuisines.
Tools Used: Python (Pandas), Excel
Outcome: Gained insights into the most popular cuisines and emerging food trends.

City-Based Analysis

Determined which city had the highest number of restaurants.
Computed the average ratings for restaurants in each city and identified the top-performing city.
Tools Used: Python, Excel
Outcome: Developed an understanding of regional restaurant performance.

Price Range Analysis

Visualized the distribution of restaurant price categories using charts.
Calculated the percentage share of restaurants across different price ranges.
Tools Used: Python (Matplotlib, Seaborn)
Outcome: Clear insights into pricing trends within the dataset.

Online Delivery Analysis

Calculated the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery services.
Compared average ratings between restaurants with and without delivery options.
Tools Used: Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
Outcome: Identified customer preferences related to online delivery services.

Level 2: Advanced Data Analysis

Restaurant Ratings Analysis

Examined the distribution of overall ratings and identified the most common rating range.
Calculated the average number of votes received by restaurants.
Tools Used: Python (NumPy, Pandas)
Outcome: Better understanding of customer feedback and rating patterns.

Cuisine Combination Analysis

Identified frequently occurring cuisine combinations.
Analyzed whether certain combinations are associated with higher ratings.
Tools Used: Python (Pandas)
Outcome: Insights into popular cuisine pairings and their impact on ratings.

Geographical Analysis

Mapped restaurant locations using latitude and longitude coordinates.
Identified clustering patterns in specific regions.
Tools Used: Python (Folium, GeoPandas)
Outcome: Discovered spatial trends and potential business opportunities.

Restaurant Chain Analysis

Identified restaurant chains within the dataset.
Evaluated their performance based on ratings and popularity.
Tools Used: Python (Pandas)
Outcome: Insights into brand performance and customer loyalty.
